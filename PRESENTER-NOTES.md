## ACT II — The 4 PM Blackout (~6 min)

### Situation
- Internet cut nationwide every afternoon at 16:00.
- High schools cut off from the universities. Rumour beats fact.
- **Tension:** getting people out once is easy. Keeping them out is the hard part.

### Vote
- **A** flash actions offline · **B** strike with the city · **C** get it out of the country.
- Same loop: `D` → `Space` → `1 2 3` → `L`.

### Outcome
- **B** is the big momentum play (+26) — real economic pressure.
- **A** is the safe one — survives the blackout, but nothing photographs.
- **C** buys attention, not power at home.

## ACT III — After the Marches (~6 min)

### Situation
- Squares permanently fortified. Someone gone from every cell.
- **Tension:** the rules get enforced in cafés and metro cars, not just squares.

### Vote

- **A** is closest to what actually lasted.
